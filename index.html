Vocalis

Current project · in progress

People with severe vocal trauma or paralysis often still send faint electrical signals to the muscles under the jaw. Vocalis reads those signals from the skin's surface and turns them into spoken words, with no implants and no surgery.

The whole system sits inside a non-invasive collar.

  1. Dry electrodes

    Pick up surface EMG from the submental jaw muscles.

  2. External bio-amp

    Cleans up the microvolt-level noise before it reaches the processor.

  3. ESP32

    Samples the cleaned waveforms in real time.

  4. On-device TinyML classifier

    Maps muscle firing patterns to spoken tokens, running locally on the microcontroller.

  5. Bluetooth output

    Sends the recognized tokens to a phone or speaker as they happen.

Curio

Earlier project · live
